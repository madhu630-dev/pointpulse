Google OAuth 2.0 Client ID Required

Why is this setup step required?
PointPulse strictly enforces authentic Google OAuth 2.0 (no insecure passwords or backdoors). Google requires your application's Client ID to authorize sign-ins and verify your admin account (001.workme@gmail.com).
3-Step Quick Configuration:
  1. Open Google Cloud Console → APIs & Services → Credentials.
  2. Click your OAuth 2.0 Client ID (Application type: Web application).
    1. Under Authorized JavaScript origins, add:
    https://pointpulse-8bbn.onrender.com
    2. Under Authorized redirect URIs, click + ADD URI and add the EXACT same URL:
    https://pointpulse-8bbn.onrender.com
    (Ensure there is NO trailing slash / at the end of either URL).
  3. Click SAVE in Google Cloud Console.
    If your Client ID is not yet in Render, copy your Client ID and add it to Render Environment Variables as GOOGLE_CLIENT_ID.
Once saved in Render, your service will automatically redeploy and Google Sign-In will immediately be active.

Operator Handbook: This guide explains how operators (including laptopmew1@gmail.com) manage assigned accounts, add new profiles, import spreadsheets, request editing windows, and claim rewards.

1. Account Registration & Sign In

PointPulse supports both direct email/password authentication and one-click Google OAuth 2.0:

  1. Registration: On the login screen, click the Create Account tab. Enter your Gmail address (e.g. laptopmew1@gmail.com), full name, and chosen password (min 6 characters), then submit.
  2. Pending Review: New registrations require approval by the sole platform administrator (001.workme@gmail.com). You will see a notification confirming your application is under review.
  3. Sign In: Once approved, switch to the Sign In tab, enter your Gmail address and password, and click Sign In to PointPulse, or click Continue with Google.

2. Navigating the Operator Dashboard

  • My Overview: Key performance summary showing your assigned active accounts, total aggregated points, open requests, and eligible rewards.
  • Active Accounts: Main workspace displaying all accounts assigned to your profile.
  • Banned Accounts: Displays accounts flagged as banned or suspended.
  • Old & New Accounts: Filter accounts by age/tenure classification.
  • Available Rewards: Browse reward vouchers and submit redemption requests.
  • Request Center: Submit account correction requests or request points-editing permission windows.
  • Download Reports: Export your assigned accounts and points data to CSV.

3. Managing Accounts & Viewing Details

On the Active Accounts table, each account row provides three action buttons:

  • Details Button: Opens the Account Dossier with three tabs: Overview, Credentials & Proxy (server tag and recovery email; plain-text passwords are never displayed), and Activity Logs.
  • Points Button: View points breakdown or update points when an editing window is active.
  • Edit Button: Modify server, classification, or notes during an active Free Editing Period.

4. Adding Accounts (Single & Bulk Excel Upload)

Option A: Single Account Addition

  1. Click the blue + Add Account button on the toolbar.
  2. Fill in the profile number (e.g. P1), account identifier (email), server (e.g. US-AZ#146), initial points, and classification.
  3. Click Save Account. The account is instantly assigned to your profile.

Option B: Bulk Excel / CSV Upload

  1. Click Upload Excel on the toolbar.
  2. Click Download Sample CSV to review the exact column format.
  3. Select your .csv or .xlsx file and click Import Accounts.

5. Free Editing Period & Read-Only Locking

How the Free Editing Period Works: When approved, the Administrator grants you a temporary Free Editing Window (e.g. 24–48 hours). A green timer badge will display in your top navigation bar. During this period, you can add accounts, upload spreadsheets, and edit details. Once the window expires, your dashboard automatically transitions into Read-Only Mode to prevent accidental data changes.

To request changes or an extension after the window expires:

  1. Go to Request Center in the sidebar.
  2. Click + New Request.
  3. Select Account Correction (to fix an account mistake) or Points Update Window (to request a temporary editing session).
  4. The administrator will receive your request immediately and can approve it with one click.

6. Reward Catalogue & Redemptions

Navigate to Available Rewards to view current incentives, gift cards, and vouchers. When your accumulated points reach the required threshold, click Redeem Reward to submit your claim for administrative fulfillment.

7. Periodic Review Forms (Admin Dispatched)

The Periodic Review Form only appears when specifically dispatched by the Administrator to you. When sent, your dashboard will temporarily stop displaying and present the review form. Once you complete and submit the review, your access is unlocked and your dashboard resumes immediately.

Sole-Administrator Governance: The platform enforces strict single-admin governance. The sole administrator identity is hardcoded to 001.workme@gmail.com. No secondary administrators can be created or promoted.

1. Administrator Authentication

Sign in using email 001.workme@gmail.com and your administrator password under the Sign In tab, or authenticate via Continue with Google.

2. User Management & Operator Approvals

Navigate to User Management in the sidebar:

  • Pending Approvals: View new operator registrations. Click Approve to activate their account and start their default Free Editing Window, or Reject to deny.
  • Active Operators: View all approved operators (such as laptopmew1@gmail.com).
  • Permissions: Toggle permissions for individual users (e.g. can add accounts, can edit points, can redeem rewards).
  • Review Form Dispatch: Click Send Form to dispatch the mandatory review form to a user (stops their dashboard until submitted), or Unlock Form to cancel.
  • Suspension: Click Suspend on any operator row to instantly revoke access.

3. Account Portfolio Ownership & Reassignment

Navigate to All Accounts:

  • Assigned Operator Column: Explicitly shows which operator owns each account.
  • Filter by Operator / Owner: Use the dropdown above the table to view accounts owned by laptopmew1@gmail.com, specific operators, or unassigned accounts.
  • Reassigning Single Accounts: Click Edit on any row, choose the operator from the Assign Operator dropdown, and save.
  • Bulk Migration ("Assign All to User"): Click the purple Assign All to User button on the toolbar, select laptopmew1@gmail.com, and confirm. All accounts in the platform will instantly transfer to that operator.

4. Free Editing Period Configuration

Click the Free Editing Periods button on the All Accounts toolbar:

  • Set the default window duration (e.g. 24 hours, 48 hours, 7 days).
  • Toggle whether newly approved users automatically receive a Free Editing Window.
  • Grant custom editing windows to specific operators on demand.

5. Old / New Classification Kanban Board

Navigate to Old / New Board to drag and drop account cards between OLD and NEW tenure columns, or use batch-move actions.

6. Master Reward Catalogue

Navigate to Reward Catalogue to create, edit, or publish reward items, set required point thresholds, and manage inventory.

7. Notification Center & Correction Approvals

Navigate to Notification Center to review incoming operator requests (Account Corrections, Points Windows, Redemptions). Click Approve to apply corrections or open editing windows immediately.

8. Periodic Review Dispatch & Dashboard Gating

  • Send to All Operators: Click Send Form to All Operators to lock all dashboards until each operator finishes the review.
  • Send to Single Operator: Click Send Form on any operator in the dispatch table.
  • Cancel Requirement: Click Cancel & Resume Dashboard to unlock an operator's dashboard without requiring submission.
  • Submissions History: Click View Answers to inspect submitted responses.

Frequently Asked Questions & Quick Solutions

Q1: When I click "Sign In", nothing happens or the page reloads.

Solution: The browser cache may be running an older cached JavaScript file. Press Ctrl + F5 (Windows) or Cmd + Shift + R (Mac) to perform a hard refresh. PointPulse uses direct asynchronous API authentication.

Q2: I see "Application Submitted: Pending Review". How do I log in?

Solution: In accordance with enterprise governance, new operator registrations must be approved by the administrator (001.workme@gmail.com). Once approved in User Management, your login is enabled immediately.

Q3: Why are "+ Add Account" and "Edit" buttons locked?

Solution: Your Free Editing Period has concluded and your account is in Read-Only Mode. Go to Request Center, click + New Request, and request an Account Correction or Points Window. The administrator will grant an extension.

Q4: How do I move all existing accounts to laptopmew1@gmail.com?

Solution: Log in as administrator (001.workme@gmail.com), go to All Accounts, click the Assign All to User button on the toolbar, select laptopmew1@gmail.com, and click confirm. All accounts will transfer instantly.

Q5: Are account passwords visible in the platform?

Solution: No. PointPulse enforces a zero plain-text password security standard. Account credentials display server tags and recovery identifiers, while user login passwords are encrypted with bcrypt.
